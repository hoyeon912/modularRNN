Key findings

  • Only the feature-style modular_rnn (raw 4-value CartPole state input) reliably solved the task — all 4 hyperparameter variants (a/b/c/d) hit reward 500 for 3 consecutive checks within 1,352–2,359 updates.
  • The CNN-pixel variant (cnn_modular_rnn) never converged across 7 runs and up to 36k updates. Several runs transiently peaked much higher than their final reward (e.g. cnn_c_bigreplay peaked 152.3, cnn_b_slowsync peaked 146.3) before regressing back toward baseline — learning was unstable rather than simply slow.
  • simple_rnn and bidirectional_rnn (non-modular baselines) collapsed in every attempt (6/6 runs): loss drops to exactly 0.0000 and reward flatlines near the minimum episode length (~9–10 steps). Two independent fixes were tried and both failed to prevent it long-term: (1) raising the HF optimizer's damping_min floor and slowing epsilon decay, and (2) adding a ModularRNN-style scaled recurrent-gain weight init (spectral radius > 1) to counter PyTorch's default init contracting to a trivial fixed point.
  • The _v3 retune diagnosed the real trigger: loss is healthy and non-zero at update 1, then collapses to exactly 0.0000 right at the update where both epsilon and damping hit their floors simultaneously (~update 2000) — i.e. the moment exploration stops, the policy goes fully greedy on an undertrained Q-function, gets stuck on the same short failing trajectory every episode, and the TD error over identical deterministic episodes trivially collapses to zero. This points to a greedy-lock-in bug in the exploration/exploitation schedule rather than a pure initialization problem, and was not yet fixed when all experiments were stopped.
  • All 17 runs were manually stopped (not run to their full update budget) once this diagnosis was reached.
